# How Do I? — Laundry Lab

Open **How Do I - Laundry Lab.html** in a browser with WebGL enabled. Click the green detergent bottle on the right shelf and watch it move into your hand. Select **Inspect**, drag to rotate it, then choose **Back to holding** and **Put back**. Arrow keys can rotate the held object in inspection mode.

Use the arrow keys to move around. Hold and drag the middle mouse button to look. Click a station to move to a close view. **Back to room** returns to the previous viewpoint. Click garments to carry them, or drag a garment from the table to a basket. Click the washer door or lid to open it before loading. For powder or liquid, pick up the product and click the cup on the counter to start or stop pouring. Stop at the red line. Click the drain if you want to empty the cup. Open and click the detergent drawer to add the measured dose. An overfilled dose creates visible residue in the wash result. Pods go directly in the drum.

The standalone HTML includes the MediaPipe model, JavaScript and WebAssembly. Gesture mode requires a browser that allows camera access for the file and a webcam. Keep two hands visible. Aim with the index finger of the hand on the right side of the mirrored preview. Pinch with the other hand and release over a target to confirm. The webcam is processed locally. Use Camera off to stop capture.

The machine routes model two example washers. For real laundry, follow your garment, appliance and product labels. Dye-transfer and recovery outcomes are illustrative.
